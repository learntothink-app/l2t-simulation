\section{Preliminary Empirical Validation}\label{sec:empirical}

\subsection{Simulation setup}

We executed the simulation protocol of Sec.~\ref{sec:simulation} on $N=1000$ simulated students with $T=100$ tutoring steps each, on two methodologies: (i)~a canonical introductory probability methodology covering sample spaces through conditional probability ($10$ concepts, $12$ skills, $25$ training tasks, $18$ transfer tasks) and (ii)~a randomly-generated synthetic methodology used as a control for graph-structure effects ($K=10$ skills, $M=3$ meta-skills, $40$ tasks). The four behaviour types from Sec.~\ref{sec:simulation}—honest, guesser, copier, help\_seeker—appear in the proportions $0.60/0.15/0.10/0.15$.
